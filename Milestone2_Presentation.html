Executive Summary

Why Decision Replay Platform?

The Industry Problem
  • • Lost Historical Context: Teams forget why architectural decisions were made 6 months ago.
  • • Repeated Mistakes: New engineers re-propose solutions previously rejected due to costs/risks.
  • • Unstructured Debates: Trade-off comparisons live in scattered Slack threads or meetings without standard metrics.
Milestone 2 Solution
  • • Decision Replay Engine: Immutable snapshot versioning (v1, v2) for full auditability.
  • • Structured Alternative Evaluation: Quantitative comparison (Cost, Feasibility Score 1-10, Pros/Cons).
  • • Centralized Discussion & Documents: Contextual meeting notes, rationale tags, and attachments.
🗣️ Speaker Script: "Without a decision replay platform, technical organizations suffer from corporate amnesia. Milestone 2 provides a structured platform where every architectural decision is documented with clear trade-offs, financial estimates, and historical audit logs."

Milestone 2 Highlight

Alternative Evaluation Matrix

Option A: Apache Kafka (AWS MSK)

Feasibility: 8/10

Enterprise managed Kafka cluster with high throughput and partition ordering.

Pros: High throughput, proven industry standard, rich ecosystem
Cons: Higher cost ($1,200/mo), complex partition tuning
Estimated Cost: $14,400 / year

Option B: RabbitMQ Cluster

Feasibility: 7/10

Lightweight AMQP broker deployed on existing Kubernetes cluster.

Pros: Low resource footprint, flexible topic routing
Cons: Lower peak throughput compared to Kafka
Estimated Cost: $3,600 / year
🗣️ Speaker Script: "The core requirement of Milestone 2 is quantitative alternative analysis. Engineers can evaluate competing solutions side-by-side using cost estimates, feasibility ratings (1 to 10), risk assessments, and pros/cons lists."
