1. Title 
Expert Decision Replay Platform 
 
2. Objective 
Develop a centralized platform that records important organizational decisions, including the 
problem statement, available alternatives, evaluation criteria, risks, stakeholders involved, 
discussions, approvals, implementation status, and final outcomes. 
The system enables organizations to preserve institutional knowledge, allowing employees to 
review previous decisions, understand why certain approaches were chosen, and avoid 
repeating past mistakes. 
The platform supports structured decision documentation, approval workflows, document 
management, discussion threads, version history, audit logs, reporting, and decision analytics. 
This solution can be used by enterprises, consulting firms, engineering organizations, 
healthcare institutions, government departments, universities, and research organizations. 
Outcomes 
(i) Build a centralized decision management platform. 
(ii) Implement secure authentication and role-based access. 
(iii) Develop structured decision creation workflows. 
(iv) Implement a multi-level approval process. 
(v) Build discussion and collaboration modules. 
(vi) Maintain complete decision history and audit logs. 
(vii) Generate reports and organizational decision analytics. 
(viii) Deploy using Docker and cloud infrastructure.
